## Financial guidance built around your business, not a template

Most accounting firms hand you a monthly report and call it advisory work. Stephen P. Pryor, CPA takes a different view: numbers only matter if someone explains what they mean for your next decision. Whether you're pricing a construction bid, weighing a 1031 exchange, or trying to figure out why a bar's margins keep shrinking, this is where bookkeeping and tax data turn into a plan you can act on.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: cpa-advisor-meeting-owner.jpg | alt: "CPA advisor reviewing financial reports with a small business owner" | query: "financial advisor meeting business owner" -->
## Monthly numbers that tell you where you stand

A lot of business owners in Port Chester and across Westchester County only look at their financials at tax time, which means problems get caught months too late. This service is built to close that gap. Stephen, Shawn Wilson, CPA, and Denise Romano, CPA review your financials with you on a regular cadence, not just deliver them.

That means walking through cash flow trends, flagging a margin slip before it becomes a real problem, and connecting what's happening in your books to decisions you're already facing: hiring, equipment purchases, a new lease, or a pricing change. You get a CPA's read on your numbers, not just the numbers themselves.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: cash-flow-review-owner.jpg | alt: "Business owner reviewing cash flow reports on a laptop with an advisor" | query: "small business owner reviewing financial reports" -->
## What's included in your advisory support

- Monthly financial statement reviews, walked through together instead of just emailed over
- Cash flow forecasting tied to your project schedule or seasonal cycle
- Budget-versus-actual reporting so variances get caught early, not at year-end
- KPI tracking built around your industry, not a generic dashboard
- Entity structure and retirement plan check-ins as your business grows or changes shape
- Direct access to a CPA when a decision can't wait until the next scheduled call

## Industry-specific advice for construction, real estate, and hospitality

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Job costing gets messy fast when cash flow depends on where a project sits in its phase. This firm tracks profitability by job, plans around payroll compliance for crews, and helps you see which contracts are actually making money before the project wraps. More detail is on the [construction industry page](/industries/construction).

### Real Estate
icon: Building2

Depreciation strategy and cost segregation opportunities get missed more often than they should, and a mistimed 1031 exchange can trigger a tax bill nobody planned for. Advisory support here focuses on structuring deals and holdings so you keep more of what you earn. Details on real estate accounting live on the [real estate industry page](/industries/real-estate).

### Bars and Restaurants
icon: Wallet

Thin margins leave no room for guessing where money is going. This firm handles tip reporting compliance, sales tax complexity across multiple locations, and cash handling procedures specific to bars and pubs, an area where general accounting firms tend to fall short. See more on the [restaurant and bar industry page](/industries/restaurants).

## One flat monthly fee, real-time numbers, and a CPA who answers the phone

Bundling bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and advisory into a fixed monthly fee removes the anxiety of a surprise invoice every time you pick up the phone. Clients get access to a secure online platform where financial data updates in real time, so a question about cash position doesn't have to wait for a scheduled meeting.

Onboarding starts with learning how your business actually operates: your systems, your software, your busy season. Stephen also serves on the Greenwich First Advisory Boards, a community and business advisory group, which keeps this firm connected to what's changing for owners in Westchester County, Manhattan, Brooklyn, and beyond. Support runs year-round, not just around filing deadlines.
