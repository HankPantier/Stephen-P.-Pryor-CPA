## A simple hub for the paperwork you need

Nobody wants to dig through email threads at ten o'clock the night before a deadline, hunting for a form that should have been easy to find. This page collects the documents, checklists, and forms our clients in Port Chester and across Westchester County ask for most: onboarding paperwork for new businesses, payroll templates, tax organizers, and industry-specific worksheets for construction, real estate, and hospitality clients. Everything below ties back to the secure online portal where your financial data updates in real time. Can't find what you need? Reach out directly and we'll track it down.

## Getting started documents for new clients

Coming on board with a new CPA firm shouldn't feel like a scavenger hunt. New business owners and individuals typically need a short list of documents before we get started:

- A signed engagement letter spelling out scope, fees, and what to expect
- A completed W-9 for our records and any vendor relationships you'll need to report
- Prior-year business and personal tax returns, if applicable
- A new client intake form covering entity type, industry, and current bookkeeping setup
- Secure portal access so we can start reviewing bank and financial statements

If you're just launching a business, our [Business Foundation Services](/what-we-do/business-foundation-services) team can walk you through entity structuring and [entity type analysis](/what-we-do/entity-type-analysis) before any of this paperwork gets sent over.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-left | image: payroll-bookkeeping-forms-review.jpg | alt: "Accountant reviewing payroll forms and bookkeeping templates at a desk" | query: "payroll paperwork review office desk" -->
## Bookkeeping and payroll forms

Payroll gets complicated fast once you're managing kitchen staff, tipped employees, or crews moving between job sites. The forms we ask for most often include:

- New employee onboarding packets (W-4, I-9, direct deposit authorization)
- Payroll authorization forms for adding or updating pay rates and schedules
- Tip reporting logs for bar and restaurant staff
- Certified payroll templates for construction clients working on prevailing wage jobs
- Monthly expense tracking templates for [bookkeeping](/what-we-do/bookkeeping) clients who submit receipts and statements each month
- Vendor and subcontractor W-9 collection sheets

Our [payroll](/what-we-do/payroll) team keeps these forms current with New York filing requirements, so you're not the one tracking down the latest version every time a rule changes.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: tax-documents-checklist-review.jpg | alt: "Tax preparer reviewing business and individual tax document checklist" | query: "tax documents checklist review office" -->
## Business and individual tax documents

Tax season document requests fall into two buckets: business and personal. Business clients typically send over quarterly estimate worksheets, a prior-year return, payroll summaries, and a year-end close-out from their [bookkeeping](/what-we-do/bookkeeping) records. Individual clients preparing a personal return usually need W-2s and 1099s, mortgage interest statements, brokerage statements, and a summary of any side income or rental activity.

For clients working with us on [tax planning and preparation](/what-we-do/tax), we build a running checklist through the year instead of handing you one long list every April. That keeps quarterly estimates and mid-year projections current, so nothing gets missed when [personal income tax](/what-we-do/personal-income-tax) season arrives. If you're weighing a [retirement plan](/what-we-do/retirement-plan-analysis) for the business, that worksheet gets folded into the same conversation.

## Helpful external links and resources

Some documents come from outside our office, and we'd rather point you to the source than make you dig for it. The IRS publishes current versions of every federal form at irs.gov/forms, including W-9, W-4, and 1099 variants. New York State's Department of Taxation and Finance (tax.ny.gov) maintains state withholding and sales tax forms, which matter most for our hospitality and construction clients filing across county lines. The U.S. Small Business Administration (sba.gov) is a solid starting point for licensing and entity formation questions before you talk to us about [entity type analysis](/what-we-do/entity-type-analysis) or [accounting system setup](/what-we-do/accounting-system-setup). Bookmark them if you like, but for anything specific to your return or your business, ask us first.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Forms, Documents & Links | Stephen P. Pryor, CPA

**Q: What documents do I need to become a new client at Stephen P. Pryor, CPA?**
A: New clients typically provide a signed engagement letter, a completed W-9, prior-year tax returns if applicable, and a short intake form covering entity type and current bookkeeping setup. Once these are in, we set up secure portal access and get started on your account.

**Q: Do you provide payroll forms for restaurant and construction employees?**
A: Yes. We supply new employee onboarding packets, tip reporting logs for bar and restaurant staff, and certified payroll templates for construction clients on prevailing wage jobs, all kept current with New York filing rules.

**Q: Where can I find IRS forms like a W-9 or 1099?**
A: Current federal forms are available directly at irs.gov/forms. If you're a client, we can also pull the version you need and walk you through filling it out during onboarding or tax prep.

**Q: What documents do I need for a real estate 1031 exchange or cost segregation study?**
A: You'll need a cost segregation intake worksheet, a 1031 exchange timeline, and an entity structuring checklist. Our real estate clients use these to keep depreciation strategy and transaction timing from stalling on missing paperwork.

**Q: What if I can't find the document I need on this page?**
A: Reach out directly or check your secure client portal, since many forms are already loaded there. If it's something new, we'll track it down or build the template for you as part of ongoing support.
