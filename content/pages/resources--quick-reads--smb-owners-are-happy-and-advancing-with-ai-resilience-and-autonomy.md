## Specialized accounting for construction, real estate, and bars and restaurants

General bookkeeping doesn't solve industry-specific problems. Stephen P. Pryor, CPA works with contractors, property investors, and bar and restaurant owners across Westchester County who need someone who already understands how their numbers work.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Project-based cash flow, job costing, and tracking profitability across phases require a different accounting rhythm than most small businesses use. ### Real estate
icon: Home

Depreciation and cost segregation opportunities get missed constantly, and a mistimed 1031 exchange can trigger tax consequences that follow an investor for years. The team structures entities and plans transactions with those risks in mind from the start. [See real estate services](/industries/real-estate)

### Bars and restaurants
icon: Coins

Thin margins, cash handling, tip reporting compliance, and sales tax complexity make hospitality one of the least forgiving industries for sloppy books. The firm's specialty in bars and pubs means these issues get caught before they become IRS problems. [See restaurant services](/industries/restaurants)

## Individual and business tax planning with year-round guidance

A lot of tax prep in Westchester County happens once a year, in a rush, with whatever paperwork shows up in April. That approach misses deductions and leaves owners guessing about what they'll owe.

Stephen P. Pryor, CPA runs tax planning as an ongoing conversation instead. Projections happen throughout the year, so estimated payments, entity structure decisions, and retirement contributions get made with enough lead time to actually matter. Business owners get guidance on entity type analysis and cash flow timing before decisions are locked in, not after. Individuals with side income, investments, or a growing household get the same year-round attention, delivered through a secure digital process rather than a single sit-down meeting each spring.
