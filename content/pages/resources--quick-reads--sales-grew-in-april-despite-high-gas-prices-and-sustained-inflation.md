## Industry expertise for construction, real estate, and bars and restaurants

Some financial problems are universal. Others are specific to the trade, and generic bookkeeping software won't catch them.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Contractors juggle cash flow that swings hard between project phases, and job costing that has to track profitability project by project, not just company-wide. We build reporting that shows margin by job, not just by month, plus payroll support for prevailing wage and union requirements. Visit our [construction industry page](/industries/construction) for the full picture.

### Real Estate
icon: Building2

Real estate investors and property managers face depreciation schedules, cost segregation opportunities, and 1031 exchange timelines that leave no room for guesswork. We structure entities and track basis so a sale doesn't trigger a tax bill nobody planned for. More detail lives on our [real estate industry page](/industries/real-estate).

### Bars and Restaurants
icon: Wallet

Thin margins mean every dollar has to be visible. Bar and pub owners deal with cash handling, tip reporting compliance, and sales tax complexity across multiple revenue streams. We set up systems that show where the money actually goes, shift by shift. Details are on our [restaurants industry page](/industries/restaurants).

## CFO-level guidance without adding a CFO to payroll

Some questions don't have obvious answers: whether to buy the truck or lease it, whether now's the time to hire, whether a subcontractor's numbers actually pencil out. That's where advisory work matters more than a tax return.

Our [advisory services](/services/advisory) deliver financial analysis and strategic insight at the level a CFO would provide, without the cost of hiring one full-time. [Cash management](/what-we-do/cash-management) support keeps a closer eye on inflows and outflows so decisions get made with actual numbers instead of a gut check. [Entity type analysis](/what-we-do/entity-type-analysis) and [retirement plan analysis](/what-we-do/retirement-plan-analysis) round out the conversation when you're restructuring or setting up benefits for the first time.

Stephen and the team sit down with clients regularly, not just at tax time, to talk through what the numbers are actually saying.
