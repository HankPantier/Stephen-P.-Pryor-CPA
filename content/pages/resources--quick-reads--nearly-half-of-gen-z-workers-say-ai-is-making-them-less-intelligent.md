## A Port Chester CPA firm that knows your business

Port Chester is full of family businesses, contractors, restaurant owners, and property investors who don't have time to untangle a spreadsheet at 9pm. Stephen P. Pryor, CPA has spent years working alongside exactly those clients, learning how their businesses actually run before ever opening a tax return.

Most local firms bill by the hour and hand you a binder once a year. We work differently: bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and advisory are bundled into one fixed monthly fee, so you know what you're paying before the year even starts. Stephen, [Shawn Wilson, CPA](/who-we-are/shawn-wilson), and [Denise Romano, CPA](/who-we-are/denise-romano) sit down with new clients to learn their internal systems, whether that's a contractor's job costing spreadsheet or a bar's point-of-sale reports, before making a single recommendation.

You run your business. We'll run your numbers.

## Industry expertise for construction, real estate, and hospitality

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Cash flow that swings wildly between project phases, job costing that needs to be tracked project by project, and payroll complexity from multiple job sites or union rules. We help [construction](/industries/construction) clients see profitability by project instead of guessing at year-end.

### Real estate
icon: Home

Depreciation schedules, cost segregation studies, and 1031 exchanges timed correctly so a sale doesn't trigger a tax bill you didn't see coming. We work with [real estate](/industries/real-estate) investors and developers across Westchester County on entity structure and timing.

### Bars and restaurants
icon: DollarSign

Thin margins, tip reporting compliance, and sales tax rules that vary by what's rung up as food versus a drink. Bars and pubs are a particular specialty of ours, covering everything from POS reconciliation to cost-of-goods tracking so you can see exactly where the money goes. Details are on our [restaurants](/industries/restaurants) page.

## Real-time financial visibility through our secure client platform

Most Port Chester accounting firms still email PDFs or mail paper reports weeks after the numbers are old news. Our clients log into a secure online platform and see their financial data in real time, current bank balances, payroll runs, and outstanding invoices, without waiting for a callback.

That means a restaurant owner can check labor cost percentage on a Tuesday afternoon, and a contractor can pull job costing numbers before bidding the next project. The bundled fixed-fee model covers this access along with bookkeeping, payroll, and tax, so there's no separate software fee tacked onto your invoice. One system, one login, numbers that are actually current.

## Personalized, year-round support instead of once-a-year tax help
