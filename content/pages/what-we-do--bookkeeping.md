## Local bookkeeping support that keeps your numbers current

If you run a business in Port Chester or anywhere else in Westchester County, you already know that clean books aren't optional. They're the foundation everything else sits on: your tax return, your payroll filings, your ability to answer the question "how are we actually doing?" without guessing. Stephen P. Pryor, CPA has built bookkeeping support around that reality, working with construction companies, real estate operators, and bars and restaurants across the region who need their numbers current, not caught up three months later.

We handle the daily grind of transaction coding, reconciliation, and reporting so you can answer the money questions that matter without digging through a shoebox of receipts. Tax season stops being a scramble because the work has already been done, month by month, all year.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: bookkeeping-daily-tasks.jpg | alt: "Bookkeeper organizing invoices and receipts at a desk" | query: "bookkeeper reviewing invoices desk" -->
## What our bookkeeping service covers

Bookkeeping means a lot of different things depending on who you ask. Here's what it actually includes when Stephen P. Pryor, CPA handles it for you:

- **Daily transaction classification**, every deposit, withdrawal, and charge gets coded correctly, so your financial reports reflect reality instead of a pile of miscategorized expenses.
- **Expense tracking**, we track what you're spending and where, so you can spot waste or opportunity before it becomes a bigger problem.
- **Bill payment**, vendor bills get paid on time, so you're not fielding late notices or damaging supplier relationships.
- **Invoicing**, invoices go out promptly and get tracked until they're paid, which matters for cash flow more than most owners realize.
- **Reconciliation**, bank and credit card accounts get matched against your books every month, catching errors, duplicate charges, or fraud early.

This isn't a once-a-year cleanup. It's ongoing work that keeps your books accurate all twelve months, which is exactly what makes tax time painless instead of stressful.

## Real-time financial data through a secure online platform

A lot of small business owners in Westchester County are still working with a bookkeeper who mails a report once a quarter, if you're lucky. That's not how we operate. Clients access their financial data through a secure online platform, any time, from a laptop or phone, without waiting on a callback or an email.

That means you can check your cash position before making a hiring decision, pull up last month's numbers before a lender meeting, or see exactly where a project stands financially without asking us to run a report first. The platform keeps your data current and protected, which matters as much as the convenience. You get visibility into your business on your schedule, not ours.

## Bookkeeping tailored to construction, real estate, and bars and restaurants

Generic bookkeeping misses the details that matter most in certain industries. We built our approach around three we know well.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Job costing is where most construction bookkeeping falls apart. We track costs and revenue by project, not just by month, so you know which jobs are actually profitable and which ones are quietly losing money between draws. That level of detail also makes payroll compliance across multiple job sites far easier to manage.

### Real Estate
icon: Home

Property-level tracking matters when you own more than one asset. We keep income and expenses organized by property, so you can see performance across a portfolio and hand your tax preparer clean, categorized records when depreciation and cost segregation questions come up.

### Bars and Restaurants
icon: Receipt

Thin margins leave no room for sloppy books. We handle daily sales tracking, tip reporting, and cash handling reconciliation so nothing slips through, and sales tax stays accurate month after month instead of becoming a year-end surprise.

## How we onboard your business

### We learn how you currently operate
Before anything changes, we sit down and learn your existing systems, your chart of accounts, your invoicing habits, and where your current process breaks down.

We build or adjust your bookkeeping software to fit how your business actually runs, whether that means QuickBooks setup, reconciling old records, or organizing years of scattered data.
