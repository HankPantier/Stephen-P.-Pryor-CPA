## A dedicated senior accountant serving Port Chester and Westchester County clients

Angelique Lecaj is a Senior Accountant at Stephen P. Pryor, CPA, where she handles bookkeeping, payroll, and business tax support for clients across Port Chester and Westchester County. She's often the person a business owner talks to most, since she manages the day-to-day details that keep a company's books accurate and its filings on time.

That kind of steady, hands-on involvement is central to how the firm works. Onboarding at Pryor CPA means learning a client's internal systems, not just plugging numbers into software, and Angelique is part of that process from the start. She gets to know how a business actually runs before she touches its books, which is why clients tend to stick with her year after year rather than starting over with a new accountant each tax season.

## Hands-on experience across construction, real estate, and hospitality clients

Angelique's client roster leans heavily into the firm's core niches: construction, real estate, and hospitality, including bars and restaurants. That's not incidental. These industries carry their own accounting quirks, job costing on a construction project, depreciation schedules on a real estate holding, tip reporting and sales tax at a neighborhood bar, and generic bookkeeping doesn't hold up against them.

She works with contractors managing uneven cash flow between project phases, property owners trying to keep depreciation schedules straight, and restaurant and bar owners who need to know exactly where their margins are going each week. Because she's handled these situations repeatedly, she catches issues early instead of discovering them at tax time.

## What clients can expect when working with Angelique

Clients who work with Angelique describe her as responsive and precise, the kind of accountant who answers a question the same day it's asked instead of a week later. She works through the firm's secure online platform, so clients can see their financial data in real time rather than waiting for a quarterly report to find out where things stand.

Here's what that looks like day to day:

- Direct answers to payroll and bookkeeping questions, usually within the same business day
- Monthly books that are reconciled and ready before tax season arrives, not scrambled together after
- A working knowledge of each client's business, so conversations skip the back-and-forth of re-explaining the basics
- Real-time access to financial data through the firm's secure client platform
- Coordination with the firm's tax and payroll teams so nothing falls through the cracks between departments

## Committed to accuracy and proactive financial guidance

Angelique's approach is grounded in getting the numbers right the first time. Payroll compliance, in particular, leaves little room for error, missed filings and misclassified workers create real problems for a business owner, and she treats that accuracy as non-negotiable rather than a nice-to-have.

She brings that same discipline to bookkeeping and tax prep support, flagging issues before they become deadline problems. Her work supports the firm's bundled service model, where bookkeeping, payroll, and tax planning happen under one roof for a fixed monthly fee instead of being billed piecemeal. That structure only works if the person doing the daily bookkeeping is thorough, and clients who've worked with her for multiple tax cycles point to that consistency as the reason they haven't looked elsewhere.
