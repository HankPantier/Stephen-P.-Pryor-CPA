## Meet Allison Jubelirer

Allison Jubelirer is an Accounting Specialist at Stephen P. Pryor, CPA, working directly with clients on the bookkeeping and transaction detail that keeps a business's numbers accurate day to day. She's often the person clients speak with most, the one who knows their chart of accounts, their vendor list, and the small quirks of how their business actually runs. That familiarity matters. Stephen built this firm around listening closely and learning each client's internal systems from the start, and Allison carries that same approach into her daily work. Clients in Port Chester, NY and across Westchester County count on her for consistency, not surprises.

## How Allison supports our clients

Allison's work covers the transaction-level detail that keeps a business's books clean and current, the kind of work construction, real estate, and hospitality clients depend on most because their cash moves fast and their records need to keep pace. Her day-to-day includes:

- Daily transaction classification and reconciliation, so every dollar is accounted for correctly the first time
- Invoice processing and bill payment coordination, keeping vendor relationships on track
- Direct client communication, answering day-to-day questions without a client waiting on hold
- Close attention to details specific to bars, restaurants, and contractors, including job costing entries and daily sales tracking
- Regular coordination with Stephen, Shawn, and the rest of the team, so nothing falls through the cracks between bookkeeping and tax season

For a bar owner juggling tip reporting and thin margins, or a contractor tracking costs across three active jobs, that level of attention is the difference between guessing and knowing.

## Why our team approach makes the difference

No single person carries a client relationship at Stephen P. Pryor, CPA. Allison works alongside Stephen Pryor, CPA, Shawn Wilson, CPA, and Denise Romano, CPA, so the bookkeeping she manages connects directly to the tax planning, payroll, and advisory work happening elsewhere on the team. Clients get one bundled monthly fee covering the services they actually need, not a stack of separate invoices, plus real-time access to their numbers through a secure online platform instead of waiting for a quarterly report. That's a different experience than a general local accounting office that hands bookkeeping to a junior staffer and tax prep to someone else entirely. Allison's familiarity with a client's books means Stephen and Shawn start tax season already knowing the full picture.
