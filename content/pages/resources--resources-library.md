## Industry-specific resources for construction, real estate, and hospitality

General accounting advice rarely fits a job site, a rental portfolio, or a bar with a tip pool. These resources dig into the specific numbers that matter for three industries we work in every day, backed by the industry pages where you can read more about how we handle each one.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Job costing complexity and inconsistent cash flow between project phases top the list of construction pain points we hear about. Our [construction industry page](/industries/construction) breaks down how we track profitability per project and keep payroll compliant across crews.

### Real estate
icon: Home

Depreciation strategy and 1031 exchange timing can make or break a real estate investor's return. The [real estate industry page](/industries/real-estate) covers cost segregation opportunities and how to avoid triggering unexpected gains during an exchange.

### Bars and restaurants
icon: Receipt

Thin margins, tip reporting compliance, and sales tax complexity are daily realities behind a bar or restaurant, not occasional headaches. Visit the [restaurant industry page](/industries/restaurants) for a closer look at cash handling controls and margin visibility built specifically for bars and pubs.
