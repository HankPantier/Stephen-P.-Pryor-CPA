## Why fixed-fee accounting is replacing hourly billing

Most business owners in Port Chester and across Westchester County have a story about an invoice that made no sense. A ten-minute phone call turned into a quarter-hour charge. A busy month meant a bigger bill, even though nothing about the work actually changed. Hourly billing puts the risk on you, the client, and that's backwards.

Subscription pricing flips that arrangement. You pay one flat amount every month for bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and advisory work, and that number doesn't move just because your business had a busy quarter. Stephen P. Pryor, CPA built this model because clients kept asking for the same thing: a number they could plan around.

This guide walks through how the subscription model works, what's actually included, and why it fits construction, real estate, and hospitality businesses in particular. If you've ever opened an accounting invoice and felt your stomach drop, keep reading.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: subscription-pricing-overview.jpg | alt: "Accountant reviewing bundled service pricing on laptop with client" | query: "accountant client laptop meeting" -->
## Bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and advisory bundled into one fee

A subscription model isn't a discount on hourly rates dressed up with new packaging. It's a different way of structuring the relationship. Your monthly fee typically covers [bookkeeping](/what-we-do/bookkeeping), including daily transaction classification, bill payment, and expense tracking. It covers [payroll](/what-we-do/payroll), so filings and compliance deadlines stop landing on your desk unannounced. It covers [tax](/what-we-do/tax) planning and preparation, handled year-round instead of scrambled together every April.

Advisory support rounds it out. Instead of paying for a one-off consultation when a problem is already on fire, you have access to CPA-level guidance built into the relationship from day one.

The part clients notice fastest is the secure online platform. Real-time financial data, available whenever you want to check it, replaces the old cycle of waiting for a call back or an emailed spreadsheet. You see your numbers the same day they're entered, not three weeks later when the bill arrives.

## Why construction and real estate firms benefit from predictable pricing

Construction cash flow swings hard between phases. A draw comes in, expenses pile up before the next one lands, and job costing gets complicated fast when you're tracking profitability across three or four active projects at once. An hourly accounting bill that spikes during your busiest season is the last thing that helps.

Real estate has its own version of the same problem. Multi-entity holdings, depreciation schedules, cost segregation opportunities, and 1031 exchange timelines all require attention that doesn't fit neatly into a per-hour invoice. One missed deadline or overlooked deduction can cost far more than a year of accounting fees.

Flat-fee subscription pricing removes the variable from an already variable business. Whether you're mid-construction on a five-unit project or closing on a commercial property, your accounting bill stays the same. Visit our [construction](/industries/construction) and [real estate](/industries/real-estate) pages for a closer look at the specific services built for these industries.

## Why bars and restaurants benefit from predictable pricing

Margins in food and beverage service run thin, and owners are already juggling food costs, labor costs, and rent without adding a swinging accounting bill to the mix. Subscription pricing means you budget for accounting the same way you budget for payroll or produce: a fixed number, every month, no surprises.

What that fixed fee covers for a bar or restaurant client:

- Daily sales and cash reconciliation, so you know exactly where money is going
- Tip reporting compliance, handled correctly and on time
- Sales tax filings across jurisdictions, tracked without last-minute scrambling
- Payroll processing built for tipped and hourly staff
- Year-round tax planning instead of a once-a-year scramble

Owners in the bar and pub space in particular deal with cash handling complexity that a generalist bookkeeper often misses. A flat monthly fee means that complexity gets handled without an invoice that grows every time your business gets busier. Explore more on our [restaurants](/industries/restaurants) page.

Onboarding starts with listening. Before any numbers move, the team learns your internal systems, current software, and the specific pain points you've been dealing with, whether that's messy job costing or a payroll process that's outgrown your spreadsheet.

### We set up your secure online platform

Your bookkeeping, payroll, and tax data get connected to one secure system, giving you real-time access to your financials instead of waiting on monthly reports.

### Your flat monthly fee starts

Questions come up outside of tax season. With subscription pricing, reaching out doesn't trigger a new charge every time you need an answer.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Making the move to subscription-based pricing | Stephen P. Pryor, CPA

**Q: What does subscription pricing mean for accounting services?**
A: Subscription pricing means paying one flat monthly fee for bundled accounting services, like bookkeeping, payroll, tax planning, and advisory, instead of being billed by the hour. The fee stays predictable regardless of how busy your business gets that month.

**Q: Is subscription pricing more expensive than hourly billing?**
A: Not typically. Hourly billing often costs more during busy periods, project deadlines, or tax season crunches. A flat monthly fee spreads the cost evenly and removes the risk of a surprise invoice tied to a particularly demanding month.

**Q: Does subscription pricing work for construction and real estate businesses?**
A: Yes. Construction firms dealing with project-based cash flow swings and real estate businesses managing multi-entity holdings both benefit from a fixed monthly fee that doesn't fluctuate with draw schedules, closings, or busy seasons.

**Q: What's included in Stephen Pryor CPA's subscription pricing plans?**
A: Plans typically bundle bookkeeping, payroll processing, year-round tax planning and preparation, and advisory support, all accessible through a secure online platform with real-time financial data. Exact scope is set during onboarding based on your business needs.

**Q: How do I switch to subscription-based accounting?**
A: The process starts with an onboarding conversation where the team learns your current systems and pain points, followed by setup of your secure online platform. From there, your flat monthly fee and year-round support begin. Schedule a consultation to start.
