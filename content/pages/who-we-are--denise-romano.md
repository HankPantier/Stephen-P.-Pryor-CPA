## Meet Denise Romano

Denise Romano, CPA, is a senior accountant at Stephen P. Pryor, CPA, working directly with business owners and individuals across Port Chester and Westchester County. She joined the firm because she likes solving the kind of problems that keep small business owners up at night: a payroll run that doesn't reconcile, a construction job that's bleeding cash, a tax notice nobody wants to open.

Clients who work with Denise get someone who checks the numbers twice, asks the follow-up question, and remembers the details of their business between meetings. That consistency matters most for the construction, real estate, and hospitality clients who make up a large share of her caseload. She's the person on the other end of the phone when something needs an answer today, not next week.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: construction-real-estate-hospitality-bookkeeping.jpg | alt: "Accountant analyzing bookkeeping and payroll records for a small business client" | query: "accountant working small business finances" -->
## Areas of focus and expertise

Denise's day-to-day work spans bookkeeping, payroll, and tax support for small and mid-sized businesses, with particular depth in three industries: construction, real estate, and hospitality.

- Job costing and profitability tracking for active construction projects
- Depreciation and multi-entity bookkeeping for real estate owners
- Cash handling, tip reporting, and sales tax compliance for bars and restaurants
- Payroll processing and filings for businesses with hourly, salaried, and tipped staff
- Monthly reconciliation delivered through the firm's secure online platform

That range matters because these industries rarely run on a one-size-fits-all bookkeeping template. Denise builds her approach around what the numbers actually show: irregular cash flow on a job site, seasonal swings at a restaurant, or multiple entities layered across a real estate portfolio.

## How Denise supports clients year-round

Denise answers questions the same week they're asked, not the same quarter. Clients get access to real-time financial data through the firm's secure online platform, so nobody has to wait for a monthly report to find out where things stand. When something looks off in a bookkeeping file or a payroll run, she flags it before it turns into a bigger problem at tax time.

That responsiveness is built into a bundled, fixed-fee service model: bookkeeping, payroll, and tax support delivered together for one monthly fee, with Denise as a consistent point of contact rather than a rotating cast of staff. Clients don't have to re-explain their business every time they call.

## Part of a dedicated Port Chester area team

Denise works alongside Stephen P. Pryor, CPA, Shawn Wilson, CPA, and the rest of the team, all based out of the firm's Port Chester office. The firm serves a large share of clients in Westchester County, along with businesses in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Asbury Park, and clients across New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, and Florida.

That local footprint, paired with deep specialization in construction, real estate, and hospitality accounting, is what separates a hands-on advisory relationship from a firm that just files a return once a year. Denise and her colleagues take the time to learn how a client's business actually runs before touching a single number, whether that's a general contractor juggling three job sites or a pub owner tracking tips.
