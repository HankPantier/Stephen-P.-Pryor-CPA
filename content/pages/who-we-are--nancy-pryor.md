## A steady, familiar presence for Port Chester area clients

Clients who work with Stephen P. Pryor, CPA get to know Nancy Pryor early, and they tend to keep working with her year after year. That's by design. Stephen built this firm on the idea that a financial relationship works best when it's consistent, not handed off between rotating staff every tax season. Nancy has been part of that approach for years, learning the details of each client's business, remembering the questions they asked last spring, and picking up conversations where they left off. For a bakery owner in Port Chester or a contractor juggling three job sites in Rye, that continuity matters. You're not re-explaining your business to someone new every time you call. You're talking to someone who already knows how your books work and what keeps you up at night.

## How Nancy supports clients across bookkeeping, tax, and advisory

Day to day, Nancy is involved in the parts of the client relationship that keep things running smoothly: reviewing bookkeeping entries, coordinating documents ahead of tax deadlines, and fielding the calls and emails that come up between filings. That coordination sits inside the firm's bundled service model, one fixed monthly fee covering [bookkeeping](/services/bookkeeping), payroll, [tax](/services/tax), and advisory support instead of separate invoices for every task. Clients also get access to a secure online platform where they can check real-time financial data instead of waiting for a callback. Nancy helps make sure that platform actually reflects what's happening in a client's business, not just numbers pulled together at year-end. When a construction client needs job costing cleaned up before a bank meeting, or a bar owner needs last month's sales tax reconciled, she's often the one coordinating the answer.

## Getting to know Port Chester and the surrounding community

Port Chester isn't an abstraction on this firm's client list, it's where a lot of the work actually happens. Nancy meets clients face to face, whether that's a quick sit-down at the office or a walk-through of a client's storefront near North Main Street. That local footprint extends across Westchester County, into Rye, Harrison, and White Plains, and reaches clients in Manhattan, Brooklyn, and Asbury Park, NJ who still want the same hands-on attention. Being close by means Nancy can stop in when a bookkeeping question is easier explained in person than over email, or when a new business owner needs someone to walk through paperwork at their kitchen table instead of a conference room.

## A team approach built around listening and understanding your business

Every new client relationship starts the same way at this firm: with listening. Before Nancy or anyone else touches a ledger, the team spends time learning how a business actually runs, what systems are already in place, and where the stress points are. That's especially true in the industries this firm knows best.

- [Construction](/industries/construction) businesses managing job costing and cash flow between project phases
- [Real estate](/industries/real-estate) owners tracking depreciation, 1031 timing, and multi-property reporting
- [Bars and restaurants](/industries/restaurants) handling thin margins, tip reporting, and sales tax compliance
- New business owners setting up bookkeeping and payroll systems for the first time

Nancy is part of that process from the start, making sure what the firm learns during onboarding actually shows up in the day-to-day bookkeeping and tax work that follows.
