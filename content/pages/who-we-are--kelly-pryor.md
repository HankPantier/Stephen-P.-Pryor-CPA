## Serving Port Chester and Westchester County clients with care

Kelly Pryor is part of the team behind Stephen P. Pryor, CPA, the Port Chester firm that has spent years building relationships with business owners and individuals across Westchester County. She works closely with clients from Rye to White Plains, and with firm clients as far as Manhattan, Brooklyn, and Asbury Park, NJ, bringing the same close attention to each one. At a firm built on service and relationships, Kelly's role is straightforward: know the client, learn how their business runs, and make sure nothing falls through the cracks. That's the philosophy behind the firm's tagline, You run your business. We'll run your numbers, and it shows in how she handles day-to-day client work.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: kelly-pryor-office-work.jpg | alt: "Accounting team member reviewing client documents at her desk" | query: "accountant working desk office" -->
## Background and role within the firm

Kelly works alongside Stephen Pryor and the firm's CPAs, Shawn Wilson and Denise Romano, handling the client-facing work that keeps a small accounting firm running smoothly. That means she's often the first person a client talks to when they have a question about a bill, a payroll run, or a document they can't find. Her role covers onboarding new clients, learning their internal systems, and making sure the move to the firm's secure online platform goes without a hitch from day one. For a firm that values personal relationships over call centers and hold music, that kind of continuity matters. Clients get to know Kelly, and she gets to know their business, its rhythms, and its pressure points, well before tax season starts.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: construction-restaurant-client-visit.jpg | alt: "Accounting professional reviewing paperwork with a small business owner" | query: "accountant meeting small business owner" -->
## Hands-on support for construction, real estate, and hospitality clients

A good part of Kelly's day involves the firm's specialty clients: construction companies juggling costs across multiple projects, real estate investors tracking depreciation schedules, and bar and restaurant owners trying to make sense of thin margins and cash handling. She doesn't just process paperwork for these clients, she helps translate the day-to-day chaos of running a job site or a bar into numbers Stephen and the team can act on. That hands-on familiarity with [construction](/industries/construction), [real estate](/industries/real-estate), and [restaurant and bar accounting](/industries/restaurants) means fewer surprises for clients and fewer basic questions eating up meeting time. When a contractor calls about a subcontractor payment or a restaurant owner needs a payroll correction before Friday, Kelly is usually the one who already knows the account.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-left | image: bookkeeping-payroll-tax-support.jpg | alt: "Accounting staff member preparing bookkeeping and payroll documents" | query: "bookkeeper payroll paperwork office" -->
## A client-first approach to bookkeeping, payroll, and tax support

Kelly's work touches the services clients rely on most:

- [Bookkeeping](/what-we-do/bookkeeping): keeping transactions classified, bills paid, and books current so nothing piles up before tax time
- [Payroll](/what-we-do/payroll): making sure employees and contractors get paid accurately and on schedule, even with multiple pay rates or job sites
- Business tax support: gathering documents and coordinating with the CPA team so returns move through the year, not just in March
- Individual [tax](/what-we-do/tax): helping clients pull together personal records for the firm's tax planning and preparation work

None of this happens in isolation. Kelly's job is to make sure the information moving between client and CPA is accurate and on time, so Stephen, Shawn, and Denise can spend their time on planning and strategy instead of chasing down paperwork.

## Why Port Chester businesses and individuals trust our team

Trust at a small firm is built person by person, not marketing campaign by marketing campaign. Kelly has been part of that process long enough that clients know her voice on the phone and expect a callback the same day. She's also connected to the firm's presence in the local business community, including its ties to groups like the Greenwich First Advisory Boards, where [Stephen Pryor](/who-we-are/stephen-pryor) stays active in conversations about what local business owners actually need. For clients in Port Chester and across Westchester County, that continuity, the same people answering the phone year after year, is worth more than a logo on a website.
