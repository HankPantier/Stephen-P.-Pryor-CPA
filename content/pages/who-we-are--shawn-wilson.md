## A trusted CPA advisor for the Port Chester business community

Shawn Wilson, CPA, is Managing Director at Stephen P. Pryor, CPA, and one of the people clients call when a tax notice shows up or the numbers just don't add up. He works directly with business owners across Port Chester and the surrounding towns, not through layers of staff, and he stays involved long after the onboarding paperwork is signed.

Clients come to Shawn with real problems: a payroll filing that's due tomorrow, a lease renewal that hinges on the right numbers, a slow month at the bar that needs explaining. He listens first, then builds a plan around how the business actually runs. That's the kind of year-round guidance construction firms, real estate investors, and restaurant owners in Westchester County have come to expect.

<!-- block: industry-cards | variant: 3-col | theme: ink -->
## Experience across construction, real estate, and hospitality accounting

Shawn built his practice around three industries with their own accounting quirks, and he still handles the details personally.

### Construction
icon: Hammer

Job costing determines whether a project actually made money, and Shawn tracks it phase by phase. He helps [construction](/industries/construction) clients manage uneven cash flow between draws, subcontractor payroll, and the paperwork that keeps bonding companies satisfied.

### Real Estate
icon: Building2

Depreciation schedules and 1031 exchange timing can make or break a deal. Shawn works with [real estate](/industries/real-estate) investors and property managers on cost segregation opportunities and entity structuring decisions that hold up at tax time.

### Bars and Restaurants
icon: Wallet

Margins are thin and cash handling is constant. Shawn helps [bar and restaurant](/industries/restaurants) owners get a clear read on where money is going, stay compliant on tip reporting, and manage sales tax without the guesswork.

## A partner in bookkeeping, payroll, and tax planning

Most of Shawn's client relationships start with a mess: disorganized books, missed payroll deadlines, a tax bill that came as a surprise. He fixes the immediate problem, then sets up systems so it doesn't happen again. That means accurate [bookkeeping](/services/bookkeeping), payroll that files on time and matches state and federal rules, and a [tax](/services/tax) plan built months before the filing deadline instead of the week of it.

All of it runs through the firm's bundled service model: bookkeeping, payroll, and tax support for one fixed monthly fee, so clients aren't guessing what the next invoice will cost. Shawn's clients also get access to a secure online platform where they can see real-time financial data whenever they need it, not just when the books close for the quarter.

## A listening-first approach to every client relationship

Before Shawn recommends anything, he spends time learning how a client's business actually works: what software they're using, who touches the books, where the bottlenecks are. That onboarding process takes longer than a quick QuickBooks handoff, but it means the recommendations that follow actually fit.

It's a habit that comes straight from the firm's founding approach under Stephen P. Pryor: build the relationship first, then build the numbers around it. Shawn applies that same standard whether he's talking through payroll compliance with a contractor or walking a new restaurant owner through entity structure for the first time.

## Serving business owners and individuals throughout Westchester County

Most of Shawn's clients are based in Port Chester and elsewhere in Westchester County, but the firm's reach extends further: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Asbury Park, NJ, and clients scattered across New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, and Florida. Distance doesn't change how the relationship works, thanks to the firm's secure online platform and Shawn's habit of staying reachable year-round.

Stephen P. Pryor, CPA is also connected locally through the Greenwich First Advisory Boards, a business and community advisory group that keeps the firm plugged into what's happening with owners across the region, not just inside client meetings.
