# About Stephen P. Pryor, CPA

## Identity
Stephen P. Pryor, CPA is a CPA firm founded in 1990 by Stephen P. Pryor, based in Port Chester, New York. The firm serves the great majority of its clients in Westchester County, with additional clients in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Asbury Park, New Jersey, and clients across New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, and Florida.

The firm's team is service and relationship driven. Stephen P. Pryor manages the firm and client relations with expertise in taxation. Shawn Wilson, Managing Director, brings over a decade of experience with high net worth individuals and business clients in construction, hospitality, and rental real estate. Nicholas Vergara, Senior Manager, handles compliance filings for payroll, sales tax, business income tax, and financial statements for retail, construction, real estate, and hospitality clients. Denise Romano, Senior Accountant, assists with personal and corporate tax returns, financial statements, payroll, and sales tax. Nancy Pryor, Office Director, has supported the firm since it opened in 1990. Kelly Pryor coordinates projects, billing, and individual tax return workflow. Angelique Lecaj handles payroll, sales tax, and income tax filings while pursuing her CPA license. Francis Moran serves as Senior Manager, and Allison Jubelirer supports senior accountants with data entry and records.

The firm's mission is built on service and relationships: it accommodates and takes care of clients, listens closely to address their issues and stresses, and learns each client's internal systems during onboarding.

## Positioning & Differentiation
Stephen P. Pryor, CPA positions itself with the tagline: "You run your business. We'll run your numbers." Its positioning statement describes the firm as a hands-on advisory partner that listens closely and brings deep expertise in construction, real estate, and hospitality, with a specialty in bars and pubs.

The firm differentiates itself through bundled services for a fixed monthly fee, a secure online platform for real-time financial data, personalized year-round support, and deep industry-specific expertise in construction, real estate, and hospitality.

Ideal clients include construction businesses, real estate businesses, hospitality businesses, service-based businesses, and new business owners, typically in the 35 to 60 age range. Clients seek a trusted advisor they can rely on year-round, guidance and advice rather than compliance work alone, and someone who understands their business and proactively helps them make better financial decisions. Clients most often find the firm through referrals and online search.

Services span bookkeeping, tax preparation and planning, payroll, outsourced accounting, business foundation services for new business owners, audit protection, personal income tax, entity type analysis, accounting system setup, retirement plan analysis, and cash management.

## Industries Served

### Construction
The firm provides specialized construction accounting expertise, addressing pain points including inconsistent cash flow between project phases, job costing complexity and tracking profitability per project, payroll compliance for mixed workforces of W-2 employees and 1099 subcontractors, surprise tax bills from poor quarterly planning, and bonding and banking requirements that demand clean financials.

### Real Estate
The firm offers accounting and advisory support tailored to rental real estate clients, including cost segregation and like-kind exchanges under Section 1031. Pain points addressed include maximizing depreciation and identifying cost segregation opportunities, navigating 1031 exchanges without triggering unexpected tax liability, understanding passive activity loss rules and how they interact with other income, poor record-keeping across multiple properties, and confusion around entity structure for holding properties.

### Bars and Restaurants
The firm focuses on bars and pubs, not high-end restaurants, providing cash flow management and compliance support for bar and pub operators. Pain points addressed include thin margins with no visibility into where money is going, cash handling and tip reporting compliance, sales tax complexity across food, alcohol, and delivery, high payroll costs and turnover creating administrative burden, and a lack of financial reporting to support informed operational decisions.

### Individuals and Households
The firm provides individual tax clients with personal income tax preparation and year-round tax advice. Pain points addressed include being overwhelmed by tax complexity, especially with side income or investments, missed deductions from lack of year-round planning, anxiety around IRS notices or audit risk, tax confusion created by life changes such as marriage, divorce, inheritance, or home sale, and not having anyone to call when a financial question comes up mid-year.
