## LinkedIn

A private room in a Westchester County nursing home can run $150,000 to $180,000 a year, and Medicare covers almost none of it beyond a short post-hospital stay. That's the number most families don't see coming until a discharge planner calls.

Stephen Pryor, CPA, Shawn Wilson, CPA, and Denise Romano, CPA break down the three tools that actually fund long-term care: insurance bought in your 50s or early 60s (when premiums can run a third of what they cost at 65), VA Aid and Attendance for eligible veterans and spouses, and dedicated savings stress-tested against real cost ranges instead of guesses.

Families who plan five to ten years out get to choose. Families who wait usually take the most expensive option available, because it's the only one left.
