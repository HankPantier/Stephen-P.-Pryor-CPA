## LinkedIn

Rent your second home for 14 days a year and charge $4,000 a week? Still completely tax-free. That's the actual IRS rule, and it has nothing to do with what your mortgage paperwork calls the property.

The math changes fast once you cross that line. Rent it more than 14 days and let personal use run over 14 days or 10% of rental days, whichever is greater, and you can report the income but lose the loss deduction entirely. Drop personal use below that threshold and the property qualifies for depreciation over 27.5 years, plus real loss deductions under the passive activity rules.

For owners of a Catskills lake house or a Jersey Shore condo, that distinction decides whether the place is a getaway or a Schedule E rental business. Shawn Wilson, CPA, walks clients through this math before they buy, not after.
