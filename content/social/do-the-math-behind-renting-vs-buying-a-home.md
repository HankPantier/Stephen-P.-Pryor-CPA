## LinkedIn

Renting a $2,800/month two-bedroom in Port Chester versus buying a $600,000 home with 20% down: full ownership cost runs about $5,093 a month once you add property taxes, insurance, and maintenance, not the $3,193 principal-and-interest number on your pre-approval letter.

That $2,293 monthly gap matters, but it's not the whole story. The break-even point, where equity build and appreciation finally outweigh the higher carrying cost and the opportunity cost of your down payment, usually lands between five and eight years. Sell sooner, after 6-8% in transaction costs, and renting would have kept more money in your pocket.

This is a spreadsheet decision, not a gut call. We walk Westchester County clients through their actual numbers before they sign anything, the same discipline we bring to entity structuring and business tax planning.
