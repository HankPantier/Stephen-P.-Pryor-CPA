## LinkedIn

A private room in a Westchester County nursing home now runs past $14,000 a month. A home health aide working 40 hours a week can add another $60,000 a year on top of that, and Medicare doesn't cover any of the custodial care, the bathing, dressing, and daily help, that most long-term care actually involves.

Here's the part that catches people off guard: long-term care insurance is medically underwritten, so waiting until your late 60s to apply can mean premiums 50% higher than if you'd applied at 55. The people who plan well usually blend three things: insurance bought while healthy, savings set aside specifically for care, and, for veterans, a VA benefit that gets overlooked constantly.
