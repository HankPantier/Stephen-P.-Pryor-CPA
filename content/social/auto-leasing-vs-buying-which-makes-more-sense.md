## LinkedIn

A construction client asked us whether to lease or buy his next work truck. The math wasn't close: trucks and SUVs over 6,000 pounds gross vehicle weight can qualify for a Section 179 deduction around $30,500 for 2024, while passenger cars get capped by luxury auto depreciation limits no matter what you choose.

That single weight distinction has pushed more than one contractor toward buying a heavy-duty truck instead of leasing a lighter one. But it's not universal. A real estate agent putting 25,000 miles a year on a car often faces lease penalties past the 10,000-15,000 mile cap, while a pub owner doing supply runs may prefer leasing simply for the lower fixed cost.

Stephen P. Pryor, CPA breaks down the full framework, cash flow, mileage, and tax rules, in this new post: https://pryorcpa.com/resources/auto-leasing-vs-buying-which-makes-more-sense
