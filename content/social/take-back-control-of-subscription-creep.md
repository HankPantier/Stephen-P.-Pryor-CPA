## LinkedIn

A restaurant POS often comes with add-ons: loyalty programs, online ordering, music licensing, inventory tools. Six or eight of these can quietly run $300 to $600 a month, stacked on top of processing fees and delivery commissions that are already eating your margin. For construction and real estate businesses, the leak looks different but hits the same way: two project management tools doing the same job, or three e-signature platforms across a five-unit portfolio because nobody consolidated after onboarding. None of these are bad decisions on their own. Stacked up, they're a bill nobody approved.

Stephen P. Pryor, CPA walks through a subscription audit that actually catches this: pull 60-90 days of statements, sort every charge into essential, nice to have, and unused, then assign someone to own the renewal decision. Read the full breakdown here: https://pryorcpa.com/resources/take-back-control-of-subscription-creep
