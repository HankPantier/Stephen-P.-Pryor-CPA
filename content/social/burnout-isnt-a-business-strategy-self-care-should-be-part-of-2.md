## LinkedIn

A Westchester County bar owner was closing five nights a week himself. Over four months, liquor cost crept up two points, overtime hours went unchecked, and a produce vendor's prices climbed 9% without pushback. None of it was incompetence. It was an exhausted owner with no bandwidth left to catch small leaks before they became real money.

That's the pattern behind this post: exhaustion isn't a personal issue, it's a financial one. Construction owners lose margin when tired bidding replaces real job-costing data. Real estate owners miss 1031 deadlines when there's no unhurried time to think it through.

The fix usually isn't more hours. It's handing off bookkeeping and payroll so you're not the only line of defense.
