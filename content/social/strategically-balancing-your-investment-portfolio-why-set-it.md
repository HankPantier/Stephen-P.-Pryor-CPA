## LinkedIn

A 70/30 stock-bond split can drift to 82/18 in five years without a single trade. Nobody decided to take on more risk. The market did.

That's the problem with set-it-and-forget-it investing, especially if you're running a restaurant or construction crew and checking your portfolio isn't top of mind. The bigger issue: rebalancing to fix the drift often triggers capital gains tax if you don't plan the timing.

Stephen Pryor, Shawn Wilson, CPA, and Denise Romano, CPA, work through this with clients as part of a year-round tax planning relationship, not a once-a-year scramble. New contributions toward underweighted assets, tax-loss harvesting, timing trades around a lower-income year: these are decisions that belong in an ongoing conversation.
