## LinkedIn

A contractor with $500,000 in general liability coverage and $400,000 in home equity might be one lawsuit away from losing the house. If a subcontractor's employee gets hurt on the jobsite and the claim exceeds that $500K limit, the excess becomes a personal liability, fast. An umbrella policy that closes that gap usually runs $300 to $500 a year for the first $1 million.
