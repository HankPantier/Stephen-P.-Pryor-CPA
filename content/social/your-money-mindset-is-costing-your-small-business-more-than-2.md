## LinkedIn

A contractor in Port Chester had his best revenue year yet and still couldn't cover payroll in February. Clean books, a bookkeeper, even a CPA at tax time. What was missing? A system for raising bid prices. He'd quoted the same rates since 2021 while material costs climbed 30 percent.

This is the pattern we see across construction, bars, and real estate businesses in Westchester County: the numbers get cleaner, but the decisions around them stay stuck. A bar owner holds a $14 burger price even as food cost rises 18 percent. A landlord buys equipment in December to chase a deduction without checking if it fits the depreciation schedule. None of these are math errors. They're mindset habits, and they cost real money.

Stephen P. Pryor, CPA breaks down what a healthier financial mindset actually looks like, and how to build the habit instead of white-knuckling the fear.
