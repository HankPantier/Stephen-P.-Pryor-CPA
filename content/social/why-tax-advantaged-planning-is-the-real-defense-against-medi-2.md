## LinkedIn

A $6,000 medical bill paid with taxed savings can cost you closer to $8,000 in actual earnings. Paid from an HSA, it's still $6,000. That gap is the whole argument for tax-advantaged planning over just stacking cash in a savings account.

Our latest post breaks down how HSAs, FSAs, and the medical expense deduction work together: HSA contributions up to $4,150 (individual) or $8,300 (family) for 2024, FSA funds for costs you can already predict, and a deduction once unreimbursed expenses pass 7.5% of your AGI. For construction and hospitality owners in Port Chester and beyond, this also matters when you're structuring benefits for employees, not just planning your own household finances.

The households that come through a medical crisis with the least damage aren't the ones with the biggest emergency fund. They're the ones who planned ahead.
