## LinkedIn

A construction company builds a $1.5 million equipment yard and office for itself. Nobody stops to ask whether the electrical subpanels or site lighting belong in a 39-year tax life. That question is a cost segregation study, and skipping it is a real cost.

Here's the number: reclassifying 22% of that building into 5-, 7-, or 15-year property at 40% bonus depreciation produces a first-year deduction of roughly $132,000, compared to about $38,000 under standard 39-year depreciation. And your own job costing records, tracked by trade and phase already, make the study faster and more defensible than one built from scratch for a purchased building.

Bonus depreciation drops to 20% in 2026 and disappears in 2027, so timing matters now. Stephen Pryor, CPA, Shawn Wilson, CPA, and Denise Romano, CPA work with construction and real estate clients across Westchester County on this kind of planning.
