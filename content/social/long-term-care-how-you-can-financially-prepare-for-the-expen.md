## LinkedIn

One Rye, NY family called us after an assisted living facility raised its monthly rate to $9,800. Then they asked how they'd cover that for another five years.

That's the scenario long-term care planning is meant to prevent. A private room in a Westchester County nursing home now runs $170,000 to $190,000 a year, and Medicare doesn't cover custodial care. Families end up piecing together long-term care insurance, the VA Aid and Attendance benefit for eligible veterans, and personal savings, and the mix works best when it's decided before a health event forces the choice.

Stephen Pryor, CPA, Shawn Wilson, CPA, and Denise Romano, CPA walk through how insurance, VA benefits, and savings fit together in the full post.
