## LinkedIn

A client asked us if her Bernese Mountain Dog could be written off because he greets customers at her real estate office three days a week. The answer is almost always no. The IRS treats pets as personal expenses, full stop, but there are narrow exceptions: trained guard dogs protecting a job site or equipment lot, legitimate breeding operations run for profit, and service animals trained for a diagnosed disability, which can qualify as an itemized medical expense under IRS Publication 502. For construction and real estate owners in Westchester County, the guard dog exception is worth understanding, especially if you've got $80,000 in copper wiring sitting exposed overnight. The catch: the IRS wants proof, not a friendly lab riding in the truck. We break down exactly what documentation holds up.
