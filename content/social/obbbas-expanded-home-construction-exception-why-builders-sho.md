## LinkedIn

OBBBA just raised the small contractor threshold for the home construction exception from about $30 million to roughly $80 million in average annual gross receipts. That change lets a lot more spec home builders and small to mid-size residential developers skip percentage-of-completion accounting in favor of completed contract.

Here's why it matters: under percentage-of-completion, you pay tax on a house that's 60% built even though the buyer hasn't closed and the cash hasn't hit your account. Under completed contract, the income lands on your return the same year the money lands in your bank, which changes how you cover payroll and subcontractor draws in a slow month.

The catch is timing. The expanded rule only applies to contracts signed after enactment, so 2026 contracts are the ones to plan around now. Shawn Wilson, CPA, works directly with construction owners on this kind of decision before contracts get signed.
